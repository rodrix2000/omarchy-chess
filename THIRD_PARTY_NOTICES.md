## Project-owned artwork and sound

The assets under `assets/` are original Omarchy Chess project work by Rudy
Rodriguez and contributors, copyright 2026, distributed under this repository's
MIT License.  The classic piece geometry, product icon, and monochrome icon are
generated from the committed source in `assets/generate_assets.py`.  The seven
short WAV cues are generated by `scripts/generate-sounds.py` using only
standard-library sine tones and envelopes.  No external artwork, font, sample,
commercial chess mark, or network resource is embedded.

Rebuild and validation commands are documented in `assets/README.md`,
`assets/pieces/README.md`, and `assets/sounds/README.md`.
